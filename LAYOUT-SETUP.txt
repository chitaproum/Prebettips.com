League pages now show two groups, not just two individual matches:
1. Completed matches in the round of the latest completed match.
2. Pending matches in the live round, or nearest upcoming round.
Every available match in these groups is shown without pagination.
Without round labels, date groups are used instead.
A partially completed round may occur in both groups with different matches.
Date filtering applies only to these two groups. Clear the date to see both full lists.
The desktop sidebar is 200px wide. Standings stack below matches on small screens.
The match list uses page scrolling, not an internal scroll box.

Standings key: green Champions League, yellow Europa League, red Relegation.
Rank markers are based on API-Football descriptions, never assumed rank positions.
