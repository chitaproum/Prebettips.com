How the predictions are calculated

Every tip on this site is produced by a Poisson statistical model, the same family of models used by professional football-rating systems. No tips are hand-picked.

  1. Team strength. Each team gets an attack and defence rating derived from goals scored and conceded, normalised against the league average.
  2. Expected goals. For a fixture we compute expected goals for each side: λ_home = attack_home × defence_away × league_avg × home_advantage, and the mirror for the away side.
  3. Score matrix. A Poisson distribution turns each λ into the probability of 0,1,2… goals. Multiplying home×away probabilities gives a full score grid (0–0, 1–0 …).
  4. Outcomes. Summing the grid gives 1 / X / 2 probabilities, the most likely correct score, expected total goals and the Over/Under 2.5 split.

KPI engine. Switch the Data toggle to KPI for an alternative, stats-weighted prediction. It builds a KPI Confidence Index from win rate, venue form, recent form, head-to-head dominance and average goals — see the README for the exact weights.

Fixtures and results are supplied by API-Football after the updater runs. Probabilities and expected goals are estimates from this site’s own models, not official API-Football forecasts or measured xG. Predictions are for entertainment and study only — no gambling advice is implied.
