1. Who we are

GoalPre (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates this website, which publishes statistical football predictions for information and entertainment purposes. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, we are the controller of the limited personal data processed through the website.

2. Information we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum. We do not require registration and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. We may process:

  • Technical & usage data — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring pages, pages viewed and the date/time of your visit. This is collected automatically by our hosting and analytics/advertising providers.
  • Preferences you set — such as your chosen theme (light/dark), time zone and odds format. These are stored on your own device (see “Local storage” below) and are not sent to us as personal data.
  • Information from cookies — as described in sections 4 and 5.

We do not ask for or store your name, email, payment card or betting-account details through the website unless you voluntarily contact us.

3. How we use information

  • to operate, maintain and secure the website;
  • to understand how the website is used and improve its content and performance;
  • to display and measure advertising that keeps the service free;
  • to remember your display preferences on your device;
  • to detect, prevent and address technical issues, fraud or abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and pixels) for the categories below:

CategoryPurposeExamples
Strictly necessaryEnable core site functionality and security.Load balancing, basic site operation.
PreferencesRemember your settings so you don’t have to reset them each visit.Theme (light/dark), time zone, odds format.
AnalyticsHelp us understand aggregate, anonymised usage so we can improve the site.Visit counts, popular pages.
AdvertisingDeliver and measure ads and, where applicable, personalise them.Google AdSense cookies (e.g. the “DoubleClick/AdSense” cookie).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings and, where a consent banner is shown, through your consent choices. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work.

5. Advertising & Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.eu.
  • For more information on how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you are in a region that requires consent (such as the EEA, UK or Switzerland), personalised advertising cookies will only be used where you have given consent, in line with applicable law.

6. Local storage & your preferences

To improve your experience, the website stores small amounts of data in your browser’s local storage — for example your theme choice, time zone, odds format, favourite leagues and which country lists you have expanded. This information stays on your device, is used only to remember your choices, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

7. Third-party services

The website relies on third-party providers to function, including:

  • Sports-data providers — for fixtures, results and odds used to generate predictions;
  • Google AdSense — for advertising;
  • Hosting / content-delivery providers — which may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the website and serve assets (for example flag images).

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete or restrict its processing, and to object to certain processing (such as personalised advertising). You can:

  • manage or delete cookies via your browser settings;
  • opt out of personalised ads using the links in section 5;
  • clear local storage to remove saved preferences;
  • contact us to exercise any applicable data-protection rights.

9. Data retention & security

We retain technical and usage data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above or as required by law. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The website is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy & Cookie Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision. Significant changes will be reflected on this page, and your continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy & Cookie Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the details provided on the website.
